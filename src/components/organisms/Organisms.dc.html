Full-screen notice
Sits inside a .camera-stage (or any black stage) when the camera, microphone or recognition step cannot proceed: centred glyph, serif headline, an explanation, then a full-width primary and a link-style escape. The escape matters — a dead end that only offers "go back" loses the user's work. Used by the timing-board scan and the voice recorder.
